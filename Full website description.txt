Full website description

Manhal is a bilingual (ArabicEnglish) web platform that connects students with universities, academic programs and scholarships, and gives institutions one place to manage the whole application process.

For students, the public site works as a discovery and application portal. A visitor lands on a welcoming homepage with a search bar, featured scholarships, a short explanation of how the process works, and an introduction to the company. They can browse programs and scholarships with filters (country, university, degree level, field of study, funding type, deadline), and each listing shows its deadline and flags opportunities that are closing soon. Every program or scholarship has a detail page covering its requirements, eligibility rules, required documents, important dates, funding and university information. When ready, the student fills in a step-by-step application form personal data, academic qualifications, document upload, then review and submit. After submission they get a reference number and a confirmation notice, and can track the status of their application on a timeline. Registered students also get a personal account with a profile, their applications, notifications, and recommendations for programs that match their qualifications.

For staff, the dashboard is a protected control panel. It opens with an overview of key numbers (total applications, pending reviews, acceptances, active programs), charts showing applications over time and status distribution, upcoming deadlines, and recent activity. From there, staff manage universities, programs and scholarships (including the eligibility rules for each), student records, and applications. Reviewing an application means checking the student’s data and documents, changing its status with a comment, assigning a reviewer, or requesting more documents, with every change recorded in a history. Staff can search, sort and filter across all records. A rule-based matching tool compares a student’s qualifications with each open program’s conditions and produces a ranked list with a score and a clear explanation of which conditions are met or missed. It also works the other way, listing eligible students for a given program. This is deterministic logic, not AI, in line with your proposal.

Users and access Super Admin, Admin, Reviewer and Student roles each have defined permissions, enforced in the business logic as well as the interface.

Design The look is built on a deep navy, teal and warm amber palette that conveys trust and academic seriousness, with light and dark modes, accessible contrast, and full right-to-left support for Arabic.

Technical approach The project is built with Next.js and TypeScript, organized with Clean Architecture and MVC. The domain holds the models and rules, the application layer holds use cases, controllers handle input, and React components are the views. It runs on realistic mock data today, and because data access sits behind interfaces, moving to SupabasePostgreSQL as in your proposal only requires replacing the data adapters, not rewriting the business logic or the interface.
